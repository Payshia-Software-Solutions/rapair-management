# Competitive Landscape: SME Business-Management Software in Sri Lanka (as of Nov 2026)

Most sources are vendor marketing pages, SEO blogs or press releases. Treat feature and customer-count claims as unverified. Dates are flagged where they are known.

## Which local and international vendors compete, and what do they offer?

### Takeaway
The market is fragmented. POS Masters is the most visible local cloud SaaS, with vertical "suites" for retail, restaurant, garage, bar, laundry, pawning and repair. Many small one-time-licence POS makers and freelancers compete on price. Mid-market ERP belongs to Bileeta, Blue Lotus 360, SAP/IFS/Infor partners and Odoo partners. International SaaS (Zoho, QuickBooks, Odoo) reaches the market mainly through resellers and partners. No source showed a single local product that combines ERP, POS, HR and multiple verticals.

### Cited Findings
**Local POS / SME SaaS**
- POS Masters lists 10 products: RetailPos, CloudCell, CloudChef (restaurant), DMIS (distribution), PawningMaster, Barpos.lk, LaundryMaster, Auto+ (garage), Creditbase.lk and RepairMaster. It claims "1395 businesses island-wide" on the hero banner and "1,300+" in its menu, and also cites "12+ sectors", 24/7 support and cloud sync — [POS Masters](https://posmasters.lk/)
- The POS Masters site contradicts itself: the RetailPos showcase says "13+ live clients" and the district map shows 51 installations, which is inconsistent with the 1,395 claim — [POS Masters](https://posmasters.lk/)
- Option4 sells cloud POS (billing, inventory, customers, expenses, reporting, barcode checkout, low-stock alerts) bundled with hardware packages ("save up to 24%"). It advertises server maintenance by the vendor and a lifetime software warranty — [Option4](https://option4.lk/)
- Ceylond POS sells a one-time licence ("no monthly fees") from LKR 79,900 — [Ceylond POS](https://ceylond-pos.pages.dev/)
- iShopMaster is a mobile-shop POS with IMEI tracking, sold through ikman.lk classifieds — [ikman](https://ikman.lk/ta/ad/ishopmaster-pos-and-billing-software-for-mobile-shops-in-sri-lanka-for-sale-kalutara)
- possystem.lk (Applantics Pvt Ltd) is called a "leading provider" by a promotional blog, so the claim is weak — [marscodx blog](https://marscodx.vercel.app/blog/pos-system-sri-lanka-features)
- Merpacc sells cloud POS and accounting ("starting from low monthly pricing", no figure given) — [Merpacc](https://merpacc.com/blog/pos-system-price-sri-lanka.html)
- OMAK Technologies offers restaurant POS with quotation-based pricing — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/omak/pricing); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/companies/omak-technologies)
- Many Sri Lankan freelancers on Fiverr sell custom POS, ERP and offline billing builds, which form a low-cost substitute tier — [Fiverr example](https://fiverr.com/pathumrandik/build-custom-pos-erp-and-retail-management-software)
- A local guide promotes open-source POS (uniCenta, Odoo POS, Floreant) as alternatives to paying "lakhs" for proprietary POS — [PCWorld.lk](https://pcworld.lk/magazine/?p=146)

**Local ERP**
- Bileeta sells the Entution cloud ERP to businesses of all sizes. It has bank and telco channels: HNB offers it to its SME customers (2022), SLT resold it on the Akaza cloud (2018), and it has programmes with USAID CATALYZE and with CENWOR for rural and women-led SMEs — [FT Entution](https://ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Bileeta-unleashes-Entution-Futuristic-cloud-based-ERP-solution-for-businesses/50-685042); [Daily Mirror HNB 2022](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/financial-news/HNB-partners-with-Bileeta-to-offer-tech-support-to-SMEs/265-240012); [Daily Mirror SLT 2018](https://www.dailymirror.lk/ict/Bileeta-teames-up-with-SLT-for-SaaS-on-akaza-Cloud-and-reselling-partnership/269-145652); [FT CENWOR](https://ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Bileeta-and-CENWOR-join-forces-to-empower-rural-SMEs-with-cutting-edge-ERP-solution/50-772291)
- Blue Lotus 360 sells cloud ERP for construction, manufacturing, agriculture, trading and distribution, services, and the public sector/NGOs — [G2](https://www.g2.com/sellers/blue-lotus-360)
- Other ERP vendors and custom builders found: Neosolution (SME ERP), Introps (custom ERP), Cipher Labz (manufacturing, WMS, retail and restaurant POS) and Cloud ARM — [Neosolution](https://www.neosolution.lk/erp-for-small-business-sri-lanka); [Introps](https://introps.com/Blog/Blog_5); [TechBehemoths](https://techbehemoths.com/company/cipher-labz-pvt-ltd); [Cloud ARM](https://cloudarm.lk/erp.html)

- IFS has operated in Sri Lanka since 1997 with about 1,400 local staff. Four of the top ten tea exporters use IFS (article undated) — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.lk/ifs-delivering-cutting-edge-business-solutions/?print=pdf)
- Kingslake implements Infor CSI; Basilur Tea is a client (Apr 2024) — [FT](https://www.ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Kingslake-streamlines-Basilur-Tea-operations-with-Infor-CSI/50-707516)

**International**
- A local reseller lists three QuickBooks packages at about LKR 1,598–3,396 per month (undated) — [SimpleBooks](https://simplebooks.com/srilanka/?p=15375)
- iDeal Tech International was appointed Zoho Financial Suite consulting partner in Sri Lanka (2025). Zoho Books costs from USD 8 per organisation per month billed annually (global price) — [FT](https://www.ft.lk/financial-services/iDeal-Tech-International-appointed-as-Zoho-Financial-Suite-Consulting-Partner-in-Sri-Lanka/42-778266); [Capterra SG](https://www.capterra.com.sg/reviews/134507/zoho-books)

**HR / payroll**
- MiHCM offers Sri Lanka payroll with EPF/ETF/APIT and bank remittance files for local banks (no public price) — [MiHCM](https://mihcm.com/en-lk/solutions/payroll/)
- Gallery HR claims 200+ Sri Lankan companies, EPF/ETF/PAYE compliance and 14-day go-live (no price) — [Gallery HR](https://galleryhr.com/)
- Humanised offers flat pricing with no setup fee for companies under 200 employees, according to an aggregator — [hr.software](https://www.hr.software/reviews/humanised)
- IceHrm's list of Sri Lankan HR software also names HR360 and RavingHRM — [IceHrm](https://icehrm.com/blog/top-hr-software-options-for-sri-lankan-businesses/)

### Inferences
- POS Masters is the closest direct analogue to BizzFlow: a local, vertical-suite cloud POS at a similar price. Its products appear to be separate apps per vertical rather than one integrated ERP.
- Banks and telcos (HNB, SLT) have served as SME ERP distribution channels before. BizzFlow could copy that route.

### Gaps
- I did not find or verify SoftMaster, Wiztec, eBizz, Hayleys-backed or Dialog-backed SME POS offerings, because searches returned nothing on them.
- Square and Loyverse appear to have no Sri Lanka-specific presence or pricing in the sources. Square does not operate in LK (unverified). Tally's local footprint was not researched.

## Published pricing, deployment and target segments

### Takeaway
Local cloud POS prices cluster at LKR 3,000–10,000 per month, with about LKR 7,500 per month common for vertical suites. One-time licences cost about LKR 80,000. ERP vendors rarely publish prices.

### Cited Findings
- POS Masters "starting from" prices: Retail, CloudChef, CloudCell and RepairMaster at Rs 7,500/month; BarPOS at Rs 9,500/month; DMIS at Rs 15,500/month; annual settlement Rs 80,000. The RetailPos showcase shows Rs 4,800/month — [POS Masters](https://posmasters.lk/)
- POS Masters Auto+ standard plan: Rs 7,500/month or Rs 80,000/year — [Auto+](https://posmasters.lk/products/auto-plus)
- A 2026 vendor guide gives typical ranges: basic cloud POS LKR 3,000–5,000/month, advanced LKR 6,000–10,000/month, enterprise custom — [Merpacc](https://merpacc.com/blog/pos-system-price-sri-lanka.html)
- One-time licence example: Ceylond from LKR 79,900 — [Ceylond](https://ceylond-pos.pages.dev/)
- DirectPay virtual POS costs LKR 500/month plus 2.6% per transaction, with caps of LKR 50,000 per transaction and 100 transactions per month — [DirectPay](https://dp-web.directpay.lk/vpos)
- Bileeta and other ERP vendors publish no prices; Bileeta cites "flexible pricing" (2017–2022 press) — [FT](https://ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/bileeta-an-affordable-erp-system-for-all-organisations/50-72812)

### Inferences
- Pricing BizzFlow at about LKR 5,000–15,000 per month for multi-module use would be in line with the market. An annual prepay discount (about 11% at POS Masters) is the norm.

### Gaps
- I found no published HR/payroll SaaS prices for Sri Lanka, and no Sri Lankan pricing for SAP B1 or Infor.

## Competitors by vertical

### Takeaway
Every target vertical has at least one competitor. Garage and restaurant are thinly contested by packaged local SaaS (mainly POS Masters). Hotels have several local PMS vendors. Tea is served by estate-focused platforms and enterprise ERPs, not SME tools.

### Cited Findings
- **Garages:** POS Masters Auto+ covers the job lifecycle, parts and lubricant stock, mechanics, credit/debtors, ledgers and per-chassis vehicle history (blog post Jun 2026). Other garage systems found were custom or academic builds (UCSC theses for Trinco Automobile, Sun Lanka Auto, Senok) — [Auto+](https://posmasters.lk/products/auto-plus); [UCSC](https://dl.ucsc.cmb.ac.lk/jspui/handle/123456789/4081)
- **Restaurants / KOT:** POS Masters CloudChef offers KOT/BOT, kitchen operations and delivery integrations at Rs 7,500/month. OMAK and Leapset (Sri Lankan engineering) also compete — [CloudChef](https://posmasters.lk/products/cloud-chef); [Leapset](https://www.smore.com/ahunx)
- **Hotels:** Boost Hotels won Best Enterprise Management System for hospitality at the Sri Lanka Tourism Awards 2024 (article Jan 2025). Other options: myhotelina (local cloud PMS for hotels, restaurants and pubs), SyntaxHotel, IDS Next FortuneNext (used by Uga Escapes) and Anand Systems PMS (new entrant) — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/business-news/Boost-Hotels-shines-at-National-Tourism-Awards/273-299240); [myhotelina](https://myhotelina.com/); [Syntax](https://syntaxwaresolutions.netlify.app/products/hotel-management); [IDS Next](https://idsnext.com/?p=2279); [FT](https://ft.lk/propertyconstruction/AnandSystems-Inc-expands-into-Sri-Lanka-with-advanced-Property-Management-System-software/10516-772788)
- **Tea / manufacturing:**
  - Agrithmics/Agrigen records green-leaf weights over Bluetooth and attendance by face recognition (Goomera Estate, 2026). FAO says three tea factory groups use it — [FT](https://www.ft.lk/agriculture/Agrithmics-powers-Goomera-Estate-digital-transformation/31-793975); [FAO](https://dvi-ke.fao.org/digital_solutions_details.php?id=683)
  - Aitken Spence plantERM (2014) — [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/140119/business-times/planterm-by-aitken-spence-powers-elpitiya-plantations-79982.html)
  - SAP for Eswaran Brothers, described as the first SAP cloud ERP in the tea sector — [FT](https://www.ft.lk/agriculture/Eswaran-Brothers-Exports-goes-live-with-Grow-with-SAP/31-797174)
  - Infor for Basilur — [FT](https://www.ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Kingslake-streamlines-Basilur-Tea-operations-with-Infor-CSI/50-707516)
  - iHarvest for Hatton Plantations — [eLanka](https://www.elanka.com.au/hatton-plantations-stepped-to-the-new-era-of-digitalization-with-iharvest-by-nisal-rukshan)

### Inferences
- Banquet/events management for SME hotels and small bought-leaf tea factories (supplier leaf ledgers, advances, weighbridge) appear underserved by affordable SaaS.

### Gaps
- I found no banquet-specific vendor. I could not confirm who serves small independent bought-leaf factories.

## Strengths, weaknesses and complaints

### Takeaway
I found almost no independent reviews or complaints for local vendors. G2 and Capterra coverage of Sri Lankan products is minimal, and forum or Facebook data was not reachable.

### Cited Findings
- POS Masters publishes customer counts that contradict each other (1,395 vs 1,300+ vs 13 live RetailPos clients vs 51 mapped installations), which suggests marketing inflation — [POS Masters](https://posmasters.lk/)
- Buyer guides stress VAT handling and LKR support as must-haves — [POS Masters blog](https://posmasters.lk/blog/choosing-right-pos-system-sri-lanka)
- Odoo needs a partner for proper configuration, which adds cost for SMEs — [oec.sh](https://oec.sh/odoo-pricing/sri-lanka)
- SME studies name software cost and a lack of skilled staff as the main barriers. One 2025 study of 60 SMEs in Kandy and Colombo found 72% reported better efficiency after digitising. Medium businesses lag in ERP adoption — [Peradeniya PGUM](https://pgum.pdn.ac.lk/?p=5508); [Malawige & Nanayakkara](https://journals.sjp.ac.lk/index.php/ijms/article/view/2241/1213)

### Inferences
- International tools are weak on local fit: they bill in USD or INR, lack native EPF/ETF/APIT and local VAT, and lack KOT or garage modules. Local tools are siloed by vertical.

### Gaps
- No verifiable customer complaints were found from Facebook groups, Reddit or G2 for local vendors. This needs manual or social research.

## Gaps a new local all-in-one SaaS could exploit

### Takeaway
There is room for an integrated local cloud product that combines POS, inventory, accounting, local HR/payroll (EPF/ETF/APIT) and vertical modules (garage job cards, KOT, hotel/banquet, tea bought-leaf) in one subscription priced in LKR at about the POS Masters level.

### Cited Findings
- Competitors are siloed: POS Masters sells separate per-vertical suites, HR vendors (MiHCM, Gallery HR) are HR-only, and ERPs (Bileeta, SAP partners) have no published prices and are implementation-heavy — [POS Masters](https://posmasters.lk/); [MiHCM](https://mihcm.com/en-lk/solutions/payroll/); [Bileeta FT](https://ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/bileeta-an-affordable-erp-system-for-all-organisations/50-72812)
- Cost and skills are the top SME barriers, which favours low-cost, low-training, partner-free onboarding — [PGUM](https://pgum.pdn.ac.lk/?p=5508)
- Banks have distributed SME ERP before, which is a viable channel — [Daily Mirror HNB](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/financial-news/HNB-partners-with-Bileeta-to-offer-tech-support-to-SMEs/265-240012)

### Inferences
- Possible differentiators: transparent LKR pricing, an offline-capable POS (to handle power and connectivity outages), Sinhala/Tamil UI, built-in statutory payroll, and WhatsApp/SMS receipts. None of these was confirmed as a competitor weakness by independent sources.

### Gaps
- No market-share or market-size data was found for SME software in Sri Lanka.
