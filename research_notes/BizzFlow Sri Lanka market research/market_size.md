# Sri Lanka SME Market & Digital Adoption: Addressable Market for Cloud ERP/POS SaaS (as of Nov 2026)

## 1. How many SMEs and registered businesses, and how many in each sector?

### Takeaway
Sri Lanka has roughly 1.0–1.1 million establishments. Over 90% are sole proprietorships, and most are micro-sized. Only about 81,500 are small or medium firms, a figure from the 2013/14 Economic Census (12+ years old). That small/medium group is the realistic core market for paid SaaS. There is no current official count for most target verticals. Hospitality (about 4,927 registered tourism establishments) and vehicles (an 8.8M vehicle fleet) are the best-quantified ones.

### Cited Findings
- **Economic Census 2013/14 (OLD, still the latest full census):** about 1.02M establishments in total. 71,126 are small and 10,405 are medium. Over 90% are sole ownership, and about 58% of establishments are unregistered. — [DCS census summary via LIRNEasia](https://lirneasia.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/LIRNEaisa-ICT-access-and-use-by-SMEs-in-Sri-Lanka.pdf); [DCS Census of Industry](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Industry/StaticalInformation/CensusIndustry)
- National SME Strategy Framework 2026 (Ministry of Industry & Entrepreneurship Development, May 2026): MSMEs are over 75% of registered businesses, contribute more than 52% of GDP and employ nearly 45% of the workforce. — [National SME Strategy Framework 2026](https://www.industry.gov.lk/web/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/National-SME-Strategy-Framework-2026-MAY-5.pdf); [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/top-story/Govt-to-transform-SME-sector-towards-tripling-economy-to-300-b/26-791869)
- A 2025 opinion column estimates about 1.1M MSMEs, or over 90% of businesses. This is not an official figure. — [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/smes-economic-backbone-or-burden)
- More than 200,000 MSMEs ceased operations between 2018 and 2022, according to the Deputy Finance Minister. — [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/120977/over-200000-smes-ceased-operations-between-20182022-says-deputy-minister)
- **Hotels/guesthouses:** registered tourism establishments grew from 4,390 (2024) to 4,927 (2025), about 12% growth. Guest house rooms grew from 18,445 to 21,463. — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/business-news/Sri-Lankas-tourism-investments-hit-six-year-high-in-2025-amid-substantial-capacity-expansion/273-338793). National registered room capacity grew from 53,378 to 56,702 (+6.2%). — [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/QZQmDo5U9dF3Yh0MkM2G). Homestays: 1,080 units with 3,231 rooms (2023 data, OLD). — [eTurboNews](https://eturbonews.com/?p=3121847)
- **Vehicle service demand proxy:** the national vehicle population is about 8.82M (2025), up 56% since 2014. — [Ada Derana/DMT](https://www.adaderana.lk/news/115789/223000-motorcycles-55000-cars-and-13000-three-wheelers-registered-in-2025-dmt). 311,592 vehicles were registered in the first 11 months of 2025, a record after the import ban was lifted. — [Daily Mirror](https://www.magzter.com/ja/stories/newspaper/Daily-Mirror-Sri-Lanka/VEHICLE-REGISTRATIONS-SLIP-IN-NOVEMBER); [Daily Mirror DMT](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Motorcycles-lead-vehicle-registrations-in-2025-DMT-reports/108-327355)
- **Tea:** about 418,989 tea smallholders (2026 parliamentary table). 2025 output was about 264.1M kg. — [Parliament](https://www.parliament.lk/uploads/businessdocs/english/23412_english_2026-04-07.pdf); [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Tea-output-edges-up-in-2025-despite-weather-shocks-fertiliser-constraints/44-786846). A weak aggregator claims "700+ active factories", which is unverified. — [WifiTalents](https://wifitalents.com/sri-lanka-tea-industry-statistics/). About 8 factories were closed in 2025 over adulteration or licence issues. — [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260201/business-times/tea-factories-face-financial-disruption-629900.html)

### Inferences
- A new 1.1M+ vehicle fleet added in 2024–25, on top of an aging 8.8M base, suggests sustained demand for service centres and spare parts.
- A realistic paid-SaaS core is the roughly 80k small/medium firms plus the top slice of micro retailers and restaurants. Taking 10–20% of micro firms adds about 100–200k. This is a rough estimate, not a sourced figure.
- Hospitality is the most precisely countable segment, at about 5k registered establishments, and it is growing at double digits.

### Gaps
- No official current count of garages/service centres, spare-parts dealers, retail shops or restaurants. Possible sources are DCS business register, provincial councils, or ICTA registries.
- Exact Tea Board registered-factory count: a 2025 parliamentary question appears unanswered. The commonly cited figure of about 700 factories is unverified.
- No post-2014 economic census. DCS has a Statistical Business Register in development ([UNSD presentation](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/business-stat/UNCEBTS/Documents/Sixth_Meeting_2023/Session5-SBR-Sri-lanka-U.pdf)).

## 2. How digital are SMEs: software and cloud use, internet and smartphones, digital payments?

### Takeaway
Internet penetration reached about 60% by end-2025, but SME software and cloud adoption is described as low, and no recent quantitative survey exists. Digital payments are growing fast at the infrastructure level, while LankaQR merchant volumes are still tiny. The government is now waiving fees to push adoption.

### Cited Findings
- Internet users numbered 12.4M (53.6%) in Jan 2025, up 7% YoY. There were 8.2M social media identities (35.4%). — [DataReportal Digital 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-sri-lanka)
- Users reached 13.9M at end-2025, and 40.3% of people are still offline. — [Daily Mirror on Digital 2026](https://www.dailymirror.lk/front-page/40-3-per-cent-of-Sri-Lankans-still-offline-in-2025/238-331925); [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka)
- Only 21.3% of households own a computer (DCS Computer Literacy Statistics 2025). — [AsiaNews](https://www.asianews.it/en/south-asia/sri-lanka/sri-lanka-s-digital-winter-only-213-per-cent-of-households-own-a-computer)
- SME digital adoption "remains low" because of awareness gaps, skill gaps and limited finance (Dec 2025 commentary, not survey-based). — [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Sri-Lanka-s-digital-future-Framework-for-reaching-digital-economy-by-2030/50-785469)
- Older surveys: LIRNEasia ICT use by SMEs (2020, OLD). — [LIRNEasia](https://lirneasia.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/LIRNEasia-Helani-Galpaya-ICT-access-and-use-by-small-medium-sized-enterprises-SMEs-in-Sri-Lanka.pdf). DAI MSME brief with 1,026 owners (Feb 2022, OLD). — [DAI](https://www.dai.com/uploads/final-msme-reports/sri-lanka-country-brief.pdf). Ceylon Chamber/USAID digital maturity study of SME exporters (2023) found "wide variance". — [Sunday Observer](https://www.sundayobserver.lk/2023/02/05/business/usaid-and-ceylon-chamber-launch-comprehensive-study?page=1)
- LankaQR in Q3 2025: about 274,000 transactions worth about Rs 1.18bn (roughly 90k per month). LankaPay reports 29 QR-enabled apps and 21 institutions as of 30 Sep 2025. — [CBSL Payments Bulletin 3Q2025](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/Payments_Bulletin_3Q2025_e.pdf); [LankaPay](https://lankapay.net/en/for-financial/fintech/lanka-qr). Press reports conflict on the monthly value, flag this.
- Cabinet approved fee-free QR payments: MDR is waived on LankaQR transactions up to Rs 5,000 for small merchants (2026). — [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Govt-waives-QR-payment-fees-for-small-merchants-in-push-towards-cash-lite-economy/44-790530); [Ada Derana](https://www.adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=120174)
- LPOPP payments were worth Rs 3.40tn in 2025, growth of more than 90%. — [CBSL FSR 2025](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/fsor/2025/en/11_Payments_and_Settlements.pdf)
- Online shoppers still favour cash on delivery. — [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Sri-Lankan-online-shoppers-stick-to-cash-on-delivery-amid-digital-payment-hesitation/108-307888)

### Inferences
- Smartphones are far more common than PCs (21% of households have a computer), so a mobile-first or tablet POS is likely needed.
- Built-in LankaQR and the zero-MDR policy are a timely selling point for a POS.

### Gaps
- No quantified SME software, accounting-software or cloud adoption rate for 2024–26.
- Full-year 2025 LankaQR totals; check the CBSL 4Q2025 and 2Q2026 Payments Bulletins ([4Q2025](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/Payments_Bulletin_4Q2025_e.pdf), [2Q2026](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/Payments_Bulletin_2Q2026_e.pdf)).
- Smartphone penetration and mobile connection figures were not extracted.

## 3. What are economic conditions after the 2022 crisis, and can SMEs afford IT?

### Takeaway
Recovery is consolidated. GDP grew about 5% in 2025, the second year of growth, with low inflation, policy rates cut to 7.75% and private credit up 25%. Tourism arrivals hit a record, but per-visitor spend fell. SME scars remain (200k+ closures) and IT budgets are likely thin, which favours low-priced subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- Real GDP grew 5.0% in 2025, the second recovery year, with growth expected to ease in 2026–27. — [CBSL AER 2025 press release](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/press/pr/press_20260420_aer_2025_e.pdf); [Newsfirst](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2026/04/22/sri-lanka%E2%80%99s-2025-economic-recovery-holds-firm)
- Core inflation was 2.7% in Dec 2025, and headline inflation was 1.5% in Sep 2025. — [CBSL Snapshot 2025](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/aer/2025/en/07_Snapshot_of_Sri_Lankan_Economy_2025.pdf)
- The policy rate was cut to 7.75% in May 2025. Private credit grew 25.2% in 2025 (Rs 2,056bn, versus Rs 790bn in 2024). — [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/country/srilanka/fragments/strategy); [CBSL AER Exec Summary](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/aer/2025/en/06_Executive_Summary.pdf)
- CBSL flags risks: the Middle East conflict could raise inflation and widen the trade deficit. — [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/top-story/CBSL-flags-economy-wide-risks-despite-stronger-buffers/26-790893)
- **Tourism:** 2,362,521 arrivals in 2025 (+15.1%), a record, with India the top market at 27%. Earnings were about US$3.2bn (+1.6%), and estimated daily spend was revised from $171 to $148. — [SLTDA Year in Review 2025](https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/Year_in_Review_2025_Final_updated_Report_2026_04_02.pdf); [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Tourism-revenue-lags-behind-arrivals-growth-in-2025/44-786819)
- Cyclone Ditwa (late 2025) disrupted the tea industry, and the Tea Board says it stabilised quickly. — [Tea Board](https://srilankateaboard.lk/?p=7415)

### Inferences
- Cheaper credit and rising arrivals support hospitality and auto-sector investment in 2026. Falling per-tourist yield pushes operators toward cost-control tools.

### Gaps
- No data on SME IT spending or software willingness to pay. SME lending-rate series are in CBSL chart packs but were not extracted.
- 2026 YTD tourism arrivals were not retrieved.

## 4. What is the government doing on digitalisation and SME support?

### Takeaway
The 2030 Digital Economy plan targets $15bn and 12% of GDP, and explicitly aims to double SME digital adoption. GovPay launched in Feb 2025 and had 254 institutions onboarded by Dec 2025. The National SME Strategy Framework 2026 was launched in May 2026. Execution risk is widely noted.

### Cited Findings
- 2030 targets: a $15bn digital economy at 12% of GDP, 95% financial inclusion and doubled SME digital adoption. The current baseline is about $3.7–4bn (sources conflict). — [MEA TechWatch/FITIS](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/08/05/fitis-unveils-roadmap-to-achieve-15-billion-digital-economy-in-sri-lanka-by-2030/); [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/250831/business-times/fintech-momentum-rises-but-bureaucracy-delays-threaten-2030-targets-610083.html)
- GovPay launched in Feb 2025, with 254 institutions onboarded by Dec 2025. — [CBSL FSR 2025](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/fsor/2025/en/11_Payments_and_Settlements.pdf); [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/top-story/SL-speeds-up-digital-drive-with-GovPay-expansion/26-778999)
- Plan to make 100% of government transactions digital within 4 years (2026). — [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/sri-lanka-plans-100-digital-government-transactions-within-4-years)
- Ministry of Digital Economy new programmes. — [MoDE](https://mode.gov.lk/ta/assets/files/New%20Projects%20and%20Programs%20initiated%20by%20Ministry%20of%20Digital%20Economy-93b0f8a4d9e9e4963a3147259bdcaf56.pdf)
- FITIS has proposed a centralised platform of digital tools for MSMEs. — [MEA TechWatch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/08/05/fitis-unveils-roadmap-to-achieve-15-billion-digital-economy-in-sri-lanka-by-2030/)
- National SME Strategy Framework 2026 aims to lift the MSME share of GDP to 80%. — [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/top-story/Govt-to-transform-SME-sector-towards-tripling-economy-to-300-b/26-791869)

### Inferences
- E-invoicing, VAT and GovPay integration could become mandatory hooks for SME software.

### Gaps
- No details on any direct software subsidy or voucher scheme for SMEs.

## 5. How big is the SME ERP and POS SaaS market globally and in the region (for benchmarking)?

### Takeaway
Global POS software is about US$14–16bn (2025), growing 11–12% a year, and SMEs hold about 60% of it. Regional ERP estimates vary widely. No Sri Lanka-specific ERP/POS market size was found.

### Cited Findings
- POS software was US$16.05bn in 2025, growing at a 12.38% CAGR to 2031. SMEs hold 60.55% share (12.41% CAGR), and APAC is the fastest-growing region (11.58%). — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/pos-software-market)
- POS software was US$14.40bn in 2025, growing at an 11.38% CAGR to US$30.77bn by 2032. — [GII Research](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/ires1601157-pos-software-market-by-functionality-customer.html)
- ERP: the SME segment is growing at a 14.29% CAGR to 2031, and APAC at 11.96%. — [Mordor ERP](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/enterprise-resource-planning-market/market-size)
- Southeast Asia ERP: US$0.80bn (2025) at 7.2% CAGR ([MRFR](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/southeast-asia-cloud-erp-market-12557)), contradicted by about US$1.74bn (2024) at 10.07% ([UnivDatos](https://marketpublishers.com/report/enterprise-software/enterprise-resource-planning-erp/southeast-asia-erp-software-market-univdatos.html)).
- Australia cloud ERP for SMEs: US$1.48bn (2025) at 13.05% CAGR. — [Credence Research](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/australia-cloud-erp-platforms-for-smes-market)

### Inferences
- An illustrative bottom-up sizing, not a sourced figure: 100–250k reachable firms × LKR 3,000–10,000/month gives roughly LKR 3.6–30bn/yr (about US$12–100M) in theoretical Sri Lankan TAM.

### Gaps
- No Sri Lanka or South Asia SME ERP/POS SaaS market report found, and no India SME SaaS benchmark retrieved.
- Market-research figures are vendor summaries, often paywalled, and definitions differ.
