# Pricing and Go-To-Market for SME ERP+POS SaaS in Sri Lanka / South Asia (2023-2026)

Method note: I ran 8 web searches with no full-page fetches, so most citations come from search snippets. Many sources are vendor blogs; reliability is flagged inline.

## 1. Price points, willingness to pay, setup fees, hardware bundling

### Takeaway
Sri Lankan cloud POS sells for roughly LKR 3,000-10,000 a month. It competes with one-time licences (around LKR 80k) and free open-source POS. Indian SMB billing apps anchor buyers at very low prices (about INR 1,800-4,000 a year). Payment terminals are rented at around LKR 3,500 a month.

### Cited Findings
- Basic cloud POS costs LKR 3,000-5,000 a month and advanced POS LKR 6,000-10,000 a month. Enterprise is priced custom. Inventory, accounting and multi-branch features push the price up. This comes from a vendor blog. — [Merpacc 2026 guide](https://merpacc.com/blog/pos-system-price-sri-lanka.html)
- A one-time licence model is also on the market: Ceylond POS starts at LKR 79,900 with "no monthly fees". — [Ceylond POS](https://ceylond-pos.pages.dev/)
- Free open-source POS (OSPOS, uniCenta) is promoted to Sri Lankan SMEs. Setup and support costs still apply. — [PCWorld.lk](https://pcworld.lk/magazine/?p=146)
- DirectPay rents a Sunmi POS device for LKR 3,500 a month on a 36-month contract. The device is free above a minimum monthly processing volume, which is a bundling model tied to payments. — [DirectPay POS](https://dp-web.directpay.lk/pos/)
- Hardware bundles are sold as packages (for example "save up to 24% on POS hardware packages"). — [option4.lk](https://option4.lk/)
- Posmasters.lk claims "1,395 businesses island-wide", which suggests a scale reference for local POS vendors. — [posmasters.lk](https://posmasters.lk/)
- India comparisons: Vyapar Growth costs INR 1,849 a year; myBillBook Silver about INR 33 a month; myBillBook Plus INR 3,490 a year and Pro INR 3,990 a year. — [Accountune comparison, May 2026](https://www.accountune.com/vyapar-vs-accountune-vs-mybillbook); [eChai](https://echai.ventures/startingup/r/mybillbook-pricing-plans--t5-su-tools-money-r004)
- Foreign POS SaaS is priced in USD, for example Odoo POS at $24 per user per month and Lavu at $59 a month billed annually. — [SoftwareSuggest LK](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/point-of-sale-pos-software/srilanka)

### Inferences
- An all-in-one ERP+POS could sit at LKR 4,000-8,000 a month for a single outlet. An annual plan with about a 2-month discount (LKR 40-80k a year) would roughly match the LKR 80k one-time licence anchor, so it needs to be framed as "includes updates, cloud backup, support".
- A hardware bundle (terminal + thermal printer + scanner) sold through a dealer, or a rental like DirectPay's, lowers upfront friction.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable Sri Lankan data on setup or onboarding fee norms or on measured willingness to pay.
- I found no LKR price lists for thermal printers or terminals beyond the rental figure.

## 2. Sales channels and what worked regionally

### Takeaway
Partner and reseller networks are the proven channel in South Asia. Vyapar reports 27,000+ partners. In Sri Lanka, Zoho reaches MSMEs through telco resellers and consulting partners.

### Cited Findings
- Vyapar claims 1 crore+ businesses, INR 130 crore ARR and 27,000+ partners. These are unverified figures from a partner-promotional article. — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/1-cr-businesses-27000-partners-and-growing-how-vyapar-is-powering-indias-msme-revolution)
- Vyapar uses a free-forever basic mobile billing tier as its entry point. — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/vyapar-vs-accountune-vs-mybillbook)
- myBillBook positions itself as cheaper and more modern against Vyapar. An independent deep-dive notes it has had no funding since 2021 and lags on inventory depth. — [mybillbook vs vyapar](https://mybillbook.in/s/mybillbook-vs-vyapar/); [Indian Startup Times](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/startup-deep-dive/mybillbook-deep-dive/)
- SLT-Mobitel signed a reseller deal with EGUARDIAN in 2024 to bring Zoho (Financial Suite, Workplace) to MSMEs. Dialog Enterprise has partnered with Zoho since 2021. — [Daily Mirror](https://dailymirror.lk/business-news/SLT-Mobitel-with-EGUARDIAN-to-enhance-Zoho-accessibility-for-MSMEs-in-Sri-Lanka/273-297142); [FT.lk](https://ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Dialog-Enterprise-partners-Zoho-to-empower-biz-with-smart-collaboration-tools/50-711234)
- iDeal Tech International was appointed a Zoho Financial Suite consulting partner in Sri Lanka in June 2025. Zoho reported more than 50% YoY revenue growth in Sri Lanka (January 2025) and held its first Zoholics user conference there. — [FT.lk](https://www.ft.lk/financial-services/iDeal-Tech-International-appointed-as-Zoho-Financial-Suite-Consulting-Partner-in-Sri-Lanka/42-778266); [FT.lk](https://www.ft.lk/it-telecom-tech/Zoho-positions-for-business-growth-in-Sri-Lanka-promoting-digital-transformation/50-772293)
- Khatabook identified convincing offline merchants to switch, and training them, as its biggest hurdle. — [CIO & Leader](https://www.cioandleader.com/?p=3363)

### Inferences
- The channels to prioritise are hardware dealers and accounting firms as resellers (recurring commission), possibly a telco reseller tie-up (SLT/Dialog) as a later step, plus WhatsApp and Facebook demand generation into direct demos.

### Gaps
- I found no quantified data on chambers of commerce, trade shows or Facebook/WhatsApp CAC in Sri Lanka.
- I found nothing on Bangladesh or Pakistan examples.

## 3. Vertical vs horizontal; freemium vs trial; churn

### Takeaway
Card-required trials convert far better than no-card trials (about 30%+ vs about 3-8%). SMB churn is high: roughly 3-5% a month for logo churn.

### Cited Findings
- ChartMogul (January 2026, 200 products): the median free-trial-to-paid rate is 8%. — [FoundryCRO](https://foundrycro-marketing.pages.dev/blog/saas-marketing-benchmarks-2026/)
- No-card trials have a median of 3.1% and card-required trials 34% (90-day window, attributed to ChartMogul 2026). — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/saas-statistics)
- PLG/free-trial median is 18.5%, card-required about 30%, and 22% for ACV under $500. — [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/saas-sales-conversion-rates)
- SMB annual logo churn is 10-14% (ChartMogul 2025) and gross revenue churn 6-10% (Paddle). This conflicts with a 4.7% monthly median for SMB logo churn. — [StealthAgents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026); [smbs.com](https://www.smbs.com/statistics/saas-churn-rate)

### Inferences
- A 14-day no-card trial with WhatsApp-assisted onboarding is reasonable, because card penetration is low in Sri Lanka. Annual prepayment structurally reduces churn exposure.
- Evidence from Vyapar and the restaurant POS listings suggests vertical templates (retail, restaurant, repair) help positioning. This is not proven.

### Gaps
- I found no South Asia-specific conversion or churn benchmarks.
- I found no direct evidence comparing vertical and horizontal positioning.

## 4. Payment collection

### Takeaway
PayHere supports recurring card billing at no extra fee, but card use is limited. LankaQR has wide merchant reach and, since April 2026, zero merchant fees for transactions up to Rs 5,000. Bank transfer remains the realistic fallback.

### Cited Findings
- PayHere's Recurring API stores the card once and supports active, cancelled and completed states. PayHere states there are no setup fees and no extra fee for subscriptions. — [PayHere docs](https://support.payhere.lk/api-&-mobile-sdk/recurring-api); [PayHere blog](https://blog.payhere.lk/?p=519); [PayHere FAQ](https://support.payhere.lk/general-questions)
- LankaQR is accepted at about 450,000 merchant locations. On 6 April 2026 the National QR Payment Adoption Program removed the MDR on QR transactions up to Rs 5,000. The CBSL says digital payment use remains limited even though 89% of adults have bank accounts. — [NewsFirst](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2026/04/06/sri-lanka-pushes-cash%E2%80%91lite-economy-with-lankaqr-launch); [FT.lk](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Govt-waives-QR-payment-fees-for-small-merchants-in-push-towards-cash-lite-economy/44-790530)

### Inferences
- Offer three payment options: PayHere recurring card, annual bank transfer with manual activation, and LankaQR one-off payment. A built-in LankaQR display on the POS is a selling feature in its own right.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve PayHere's percentage rates.
- LankaQR cannot do recurring billing, as far as I found.

## 5. Language, offline capability, local support

### Takeaway
Vernacular UI was central to Khatabook's growth (12-13 languages). Offline-first support is commonly claimed but weakly sourced.

### Cited Findings
- Khatabook's CEO called language inclusion a major scaling factor. The app has 5 crore+ merchants across 13 languages (company figures). — [CIO & Leader](https://www.cioandleader.com/?p=3363); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2021/08/product-roadmap-fintech-startup-khatabook-10-million-msme-users/amp)
- Khatabook offers offline entry with auto-sync. This comes from a low-quality source. — [Miracuves](https://miracuves.com/blog/what-is-khatabook-and-how-does-it-work/)
- Sri Lankan research ties adoption of computerized accounting systems to accounting expertise and technological capability. — [Manufacturing SME study, 2025](https://reference-global.com/article/10.4038/ijcbr.v3i2.20)

### Inferences
- Sinhala and Tamil UI plus receipts, offline POS mode and WhatsApp support in local languages are likely differentiators against Zoho and Odoo.

### Gaps
- I found no Sri Lanka-specific survey on language or offline preference.
